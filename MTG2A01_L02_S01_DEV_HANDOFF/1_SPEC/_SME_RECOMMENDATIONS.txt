  [body] Developer Notes:
  [body] Structure: Three counting levels—2s, 5s and 10s—with three rounds in each.
  [body] Starting point: Always prefill the first pod. Prefill one additional pod, varying its position between rounds. Prefilled animals cannot be moved.
  [body] Number logic: Generate each sequence using start + position × step. For 2s, include both odd-start and even-start sequences. Keep numbers within 100.
  [body] Animal consistency: Keep the same animal cast throughout the three rounds of one counting level. Introduce a different cast when the child chooses another counting level. Correct and distractor animals should use the same visual treatment.
  [body] Distractors: Begin with one extra animal; use two in the final round. Distractor numbers must not belong to the five-number sequence. Shuffle the ground positions each round.
  [body] Movement: Ground animals may gently bob or look around. Keep their positions stable during dragging so targets remain easy to select.
  [body] Progression: Move automatically between rounds. Return to the menu only after all three rounds of the selected counting level are complete.
  [body] VO: Update the starting number and counting step for each round. Say only the selected number after each correct drop.
  [body] UI: Keep the game full screen without a permanent instruction bar.
